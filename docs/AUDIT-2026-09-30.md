# TS-Pico firmware: assumption audit (2026-09-30)

**Scope:** every hardcoded value, threshold, timeout, retry count, guard and workaround in `src/TS/*.py`, `src/main.py`, `src/manifest.py` and `src/config.ini`, checked for assumptions that may have outlived their reason. Style and general optimisation were out of scope.

**How it was done:**
- Six parallel passes, each over one slice and each in its own worktree:
  - `tspico.py`, in four slices;
  - `tspico_io.py`;
  - the small modules (`sdcard`, `channels`, `catalog`, `printer`, `native`, `extcmd`, `main.py`, `manifest.py`, `config.ini`).
- Each item's origin was traced through `git blame`. Two commits that touched every line were skipped: `--ignore-rev c897e91` (#57, line endings) and the #13/#25 moves. PR and issue text was read where there was one.
- Anything older than git history (`b2758a5`, 2026-05-03) was traced through `archive/tspico-ryan.py` and its changelog.
- Experiments were edits followed by the 34 host tests on Python 3.13, then reverted. Nothing was committed, and the Pico was not touched.

**Limits:**
- **"Tests pass" is not treated as evidence on its own.** Large parts of the firmware have no host coverage: the `TS2068_IO` dispatch, `ZX48_IO`, `GETLOG`, `CDIR`, `FWD`/`REW`, `COPY_FILE`, `PROCESS_ASM`, the old-ROM paths and SEND_MSG2's keyword widths.
- **The `tspico_io.py` pass ran no experiments.** The auto-mode permission classifier blocked its edits and some of its greps and blames. Its items rest on reading the code.
- **Line numbers** are for `main` (2d134dd). On branch `claude/send-msg2-line-tracking-e3c55b`, `tspico.py` lines after 1818 are 2 lower.
- **Nothing here was observed on hardware.** "Reproduced" means reproduced on the host with the test fakes.

### Item 11: how NOT to drive it, and where the next attempt should start

Attempted 2026-10-01 and abandoned. A BASIC `LOAD` cannot present TADDR >= 10
-- the pre-header's TADDR byte is the low byte of the stock `T-ADDR` sysvar
(`0x5C74`), and a BASIC LOAD always leaves 1-3 -- so the check needs machine
code calling the ROM's LD_BYTES. The obvious route does not work:

- EXROM `0x00FC` really is `C3 6D 19` (`JP 196Dh`), the LD_BYTES entry, at file
  offset `0x40FC` of the 32K `src/rom/TSPICO-21.ROM`. But that is its address
  **with the EXROM paged in**. From BASIC the HOME bank holds `0x0000-0x3FFF`,
  and HOME `0x00FC` is keyword-table text (`A4 4E 4F D4`, "NOT"/"BIN" with the
  high bits set). `CALL 00FCh` from a REM stub executes character data: the
  2068 resets, and the Pico logs nothing, because the Z80 dies before any
  pre-header goes out.
- The bank switch is not optional and is not a two-instruction job. `OUT
  (0F4h)` is the HSR, one bit per 8K chunk (`LD A,3` = chunks 0+1), and port
  `0xFF` bit 7 picks EXROM over DOCK ([MEMORY_MAP.md](rom-analysis/MEMORY_MAP.md)).
  `nofile.bas`'s line-1 stub does exactly this for a `LD A,(1853h)` peek.
- The sanctioned route is the thunk: `CALL_EXROM` at HOME `0x3CE3`
  (`EXX; LD HL,<target>; JP 3CE3`) with a returning variant `CALL_EXROM_RET` at
  `0x03FC` ([SYMBOLS.md](rom-analysis/SYMBOLS.md)). `0x03FC` pushes a four-word
  frame (`FEFCh`, 0, 0), takes its real target from sysvar `0x5DCD`, and
  dispatches through RAM-resident code at `0xFD90` / `0x65D0`, `LDIR`'d out of
  EXROM `0x1000-0x162F` at boot. Its two documented callers are BEEPER and the
  screen dump, whose register contracts are much simpler than LD_BYTES'
  (A = flag, carry = LOAD, IX = destination, DE = length).

So the next attempt starts by establishing whether `AF`, `IX` and `DE` survive
`CALL_EXROM_RET`, not by calling `0x00FC`. An alternative worth weighing first:
find a real program whose loader already does a headerless LD_BYTES, and load
that -- it exercises the path with no custom code and no new ROM knowledge.

Item 7 on `batch-b-protocol` does not depend on any of this. It widens the
condition so every flag-`00`/`FF` pre-header reaches its handler, which raises
READY itself (#64), and `load_ts_hosttest.py` pins that no `pre[1] < 10`
exception is left. What stays unconfirmed is item 11's **premise** -- that a
headerless LOAD with TADDR >= 10 is reachable in practice and did misbehave.
