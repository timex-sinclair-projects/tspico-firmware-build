BASIC Command             BORDER
--------------------------------
BORDER color

Set the border color and paper
color for the lower lines of the
screen. Error K if not 0..7. It
doesn't support the pseudo-
colors 8 and 9.

Colors:
0 Black    3 Magenta    6 Yellow
1 Blue     4 Green      7 White
2 Red      5 Cyan

See also: PAPER, INK
