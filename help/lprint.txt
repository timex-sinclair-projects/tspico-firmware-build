BASIC Command             LPRINT
--------------------------------
LPRINT [prItem[<sep>prItem ...]]

Print the prItems to the printer
via stream #3. Multiple items
are separated by <sep> character
which is a semicolon, comma, or
an apostrophe. The prItems are
most of the possibilities that
PRINT supports, except that ink
pixels are printed black and 
paper pixels left as white even
if the printing ink color is 
white and paper color is black.

If the TS-Pico is in "ts2040" 
(hardware print) mode, print to
the printer, otherwise, in 
"picopt" (software print) mode,
printing is sent to the TS-Pico.

See also: PRINT, picopt, ts2040, streams
