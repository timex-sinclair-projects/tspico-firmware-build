BASIC Command               BEEP
--------------------------------
BEEP duration,pitch

Play a note through the internal
speaker of a given pitch value
of semitones above or below
middle C for the specified
duration in seconds. The tones
can be heard from the speaker
and through the MIC socket. 10
seconds is the longest duration,
and the pitch ranges from -60 to
69. Ex.: BEEP 0.2,9
