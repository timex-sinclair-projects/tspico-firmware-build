BASIC Command               COPY
--------------------------------
COPY

Prints the top 22 lines of the
diplay on the printer. You can't
COPY a screen of an automatic
listing. Since it is monochrome,
pixels that are INK color
(binary 1 in memory) appear
black with PAPER pixels (binary
0 in memory) as the white paper.
Things printed with INVERSE will
appear so, even if the colors
make it look normal as INVERSE
causes the PAPER pixels to be
set to binary 1 in memory and
INK pixels as binary 0. Pressing
BREAK will stop the printing.

With a TS-Pico in "ts2040"
(hardware print) mode, the 
output goes to the printer. In
"picopt" (software print) mode,
the output goes to the TS-Pico,
but this currently is not
implemented.

See also: LPRINT, ts2040, picopt
