BASIC Command              SOUND
--------------------------------
SOUND reg,val[;reg,val ...]

This controls the sound chip
output by setting the values of
the chip's control registers.
This is separate from the BEEP
output, although they share the
one speaker.

  1 REM Freq. to coarse and fine
 10 LET freq=440
 20 LET x=1750000/(16*freq)
 30 LET coarse=INT (x/256)
 40 LET fine=INT x-coarse*256
 50 SOUND 0,fine;1,coarse;7,62;
          8,15
 60 PAUSE 20: SOUND 8,0

Reg Function               Value
--- ---------------------- -----
 0  Ch. A fine tune        0-255
 1  Ch. A coarse tune      0- 15
 2  Ch. B fine tune        0-255
 3  Ch. B coarse tune      0- 15
 4  Ch. C fine tune        0-255
 5  Ch. C coarse tune      0- 15
 6  Noise freq. high->low  0- 31
 7  Enable ch. or noise:   0- 63
  Tone  A=1, B= 2, C= 4 (subtr.
  Noise A=8, B=16, C=32 from 63)
 8  A volume, 16=envelope  0- 16
 9  B volume, 16=envelope  0- 16
10  C volume, 16=envelope  0- 16
11  Envelope period fine   0-255
12  Envelope period coarse 0- 15
13  Envelope shape:        0- 15
  Hold      1  Attack   4 (add
  Alternate 2  Continue 8  to 0)
