BASIC Command             CIRCLE
--------------------------------
CIRCLE [attr;...;]x,y,r

Draw a circle at center x,y with
radius r. Just like PLOT and 
DRAW, it draws with the current
INK and PAPER colors as well as
the current OVER, BRIGHT, and
INVERSE settings. Optionally,
you can include attributes like
with PRINT to override permanent
attributes.

See also: PLOT, DRAW, attributes
