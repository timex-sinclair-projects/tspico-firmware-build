TS-Pico 2.3 is out: firmware 2.3 with ROM 2.3. The two go together, so install both; the
updater does it in one run.

ROM 2.3 changes how the 2068 and the TS-Pico share a conversation:

- **The TS-Pico decides what a key means.** At a "Scroll?" prompt, a menu or a yes/no
  question, the 2068 now passes every key to the TS-Pico, and `n` works as well as `N`.
  The one change you'll notice is in `ERASE "*.bak"` and other patterns: **N** now skips
  that file and goes on to the next, instead of stopping the questions.
- **Every colour gets through.** Text from the TS-Pico can now carry black ink, magenta
  paper, FLASH and the rest, and the 2068's block graphics, UDGs and keywords. Before, any
  of them cut the text short.
- **The ROM says which ROM it is.** `SAVE "tpi:info"` shows the version your 2068 is really
  running.

Also new since 2.2: **TS-Pico-Updater.exe** for Windows, where the browser can't write the
firmware; the [user manual]({{ '/manual/user-manual.html' | relative_url }}) as PDFs to read
or print as a booklet; and a guide to
[testing TS-Pico programs in an emulator]({{ '/manual/emulators.html' | relative_url }}).
The [release notes](https://github.com/timex-sinclair-projects/tspico-firmware-build/releases/tag/v2.3)
have the details, including what machine-code programmers need to change.

## Getting 2.3

Use the [web updater]({{ site.updater_url | relative_url }}) in Chrome or Edge with the
TS-2068 switched off, and choose **Latest release**. On Windows, run **TS-Pico-Updater.exe**
from the [latest release](https://github.com/timex-sinclair-projects/tspico-firmware-build/releases/latest).
Leave **Update the TS-2068 ROM** ticked: firmware 2.3 needs ROM 2.3. You'll type two commands
on the 2068 while it writes the ROM. The update erases the Pico's own files first and puts
fresh ones back, so `config.ini` settings return to their defaults. The SD card is not
touched. Coming from 1.1, read
[What's new since 1.1]({{ '/updates/whats-new-since-1-1/' | relative_url }}) first.

Afterwards, `PRINT PEEK 101` gives **35** on ROM 2.3, and `SAVE "tpi:info"` shows firmware
2.3 and ROM 2.3.
