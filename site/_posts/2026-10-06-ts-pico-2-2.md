TS-Pico 2.2 is out: firmware 2.2 with ROM 2.2. It is a release of fixes, most of them found
while writing the [programmer's reference](https://github.com/timex-sinclair-projects/tspico-firmware-build/tree/main/docs/reference),
which explains the firmware and the ROM function by function.

ROM 2.2 is ROM 2.1 with two fixes:

- `tpi:tape` no longer turns off printing to the TS-Pico. It changes only the LOAD/SAVE
  switch, like `tpi:sdcard`.
- `f:` files and `OPEN #` channels give **J** at once when no TS-Pico answers, instead of
  waiting for the timeout.

The firmware fixes include `tpi:blkrcv`, which now refuses unless a ROM image is mounted;
`tpi:log` on a new Pico; `tpi:newtap` with a dot in the name; `tpi:md`, which now takes
one folder name and refuses the rest; and clearer messages from `tpi:dir`, `tpi:ffw` and
`tpi:dock`. The [release notes](https://github.com/timex-sinclair-projects/tspico-firmware-build/releases/tag/v2.2)
list them all.

## Getting 2.2

Use the [web updater]({{ site.updater_url | relative_url }}) in Chrome or Edge with the
TS-2068 switched off, and choose **Latest release**. Firmware 2.2 also works with ROM 2.1,
so on a board already on 2.1 the page updates the firmware only. Tick **Update the TS-2068
ROM** to get ROM 2.2 as well; that erases the Pico's own files and puts fresh ones back, so
`config.ini` settings return to their defaults. Coming from 1.1, read
[What's new since 1.1]({{ '/updates/whats-new-since-1-1/' | relative_url }}) first.

Afterwards, `PRINT PEEK 101` gives **34** on ROM 2.2, and `SAVE "tpi:info"` shows firmware 2.2.
