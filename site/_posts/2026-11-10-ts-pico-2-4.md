TS-Pico 2.4 is out: firmware 2.4 with ROM 2.4. The two go together, so install both; the
updater does it in one run. A TS-Pico on 2.3 needs the new ROM too.

ROM 2.4 gives the 2068's own keywords more to do:

- **COPY copies, and MOVE renames.** `COPY "multi.tap" TO "games"` copies a file;
  `MOVE "songs.tap" TO "tunes.tap"` renames it or moves it to another folder.
  `MOVE TO "games"` still goes into a folder.
- **FORMAT "name.tap" starts a new, empty tape**, mounted and ready to SAVE onto.
- **LIST "notes.txt" shows a file** on the screen, as text or as a hex dump.
- **RESTORE #4 goes back to the start of a file** without closing it.
- **CAT #4 writes a listing into a file**, and **OPEN #4,"x:"** lets a program ask the
  TS-Pico which folder it's in, what's mounted and how much room is left on the card.
- **The card can have a name.** `FORMAT ":GAMES"` labels it, and CAT shows the label.

The firmware lists folders of up to 300 files, in color, on a 2.x board. SAVE onto the
mounted tape mounts the new file, colored text can't vanish into the paper, and `.DCK`
cartridges load more reliably. The menu you get from `LOAD ""` with nothing mounted has a
one-line command menu (**T**) and ULA Plus settings for the Pico Video board (**V**).

For the v3 card, 2.4 adds the TS2050 modem, speech from the SP0256-AL2, tapes downloaded
over WiFi from archive.org, a Kempston joystick and the card's 8 MB PSRAM.

The manuals have grown too: a
[Quick Start Guide]({{ '/manual/quick-start.html' | relative_url }}), a Quick Reference Guide
in the style of the one that came with the 2068, and new drawings in the
[user manual]({{ '/manual/user-manual.html' | relative_url }}).
The [release notes](https://github.com/timex-sinclair-projects/tspico-firmware-build/releases/tag/v2.4)
have the details, including what machine-code programmers need to change.

## Getting 2.4

Use the [web updater]({{ site.updater_url | relative_url }}) in Chrome or Edge with the
TS-2068 switched off, and choose **Latest release**. On Windows, run **TS-Pico-Updater.exe**
from the [latest release](https://github.com/timex-sinclair-projects/tspico-firmware-build/releases/latest).
Leave **Update the TS-2068 ROM** ticked: firmware 2.4 needs ROM 2.4. You'll type two commands
on the 2068 while it writes the ROM. The update erases the Pico's own files first and puts
fresh ones back, so `config.ini` settings return to their defaults. The SD card is not
touched. Coming from 1.1, read
[What's new since 1.1]({{ '/updates/whats-new-since-1-1/' | relative_url }}) first.

Afterwards, `PRINT PEEK 101` gives **36** on ROM 2.4, and `SAVE "tpi:info"` shows firmware
2.4 and ROM 2.4.
