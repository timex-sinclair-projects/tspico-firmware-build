TS-Pico Command            LABEL
--------------------------------
SAVE "tpi:label"
Shows the SD card's label, the
name a PC or Mac shows for it.

SAVE "tpi:label GAMES"
Sets it: up to 11 characters,
kept in upper case. Not allowed
(Report Q): a space first, the
characters the 2068 can't show,
or . " * + , / : ; < = > ? [ \ ]

SAVE "tpi:label -"
Takes the label off.

FORMAT ":GAMES" and FORMAT ":"
do the same from BASIC.

CAT shows the label in place of
"SD" on the card's line. A label
changed on a computer shows when
the card is back in the TS-Pico.

See also: format, cat
