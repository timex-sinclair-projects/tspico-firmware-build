BASIC Command              PAPER
--------------------------------
PAPER color

Set the paper color to be used
by subsequent printing. Error K
if the value is not 0..9.
The transparent color (8) uses
the existing color at the 
location of the printing. The
contrasting color (9) uses white
or black paper to contrast with
the existing ink color at the
printing location. The paper
color of the lower lines is set
by the border color. The paper
color does not affect printed
output.
  PAPER 1

Colors:
0 Black    3 Magenta    6 Yellow
1 Blue     4 Green      7 White
2 Red      5 Cyan

8 Transparent (existing paper)
9 Contrasting (black or white)

BASIC Attribute            PAPER
--------------------------------
This sets the temporary paper
color within a PRINT statement.
  PRINT PAPER 1;"Blue"

See also: INK, BORDER,          attributes
