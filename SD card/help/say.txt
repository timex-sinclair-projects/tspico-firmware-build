TS-Pico Command              SAY
--------------------------------
SAVE "tpi:say <text>"
SAVE "tpi:say [allo1 allo2 ...]"
SAVE "tpi:say"

*v3 board only*
Speaks the text or allophones
given. Give no text to stop
speaking. The speech will
continue in the background, and 
any other say commands will have
their speech wait in a queue.

SAVE "tpi:say HELLO, THERE"
SAVE "tpi:say [HH1 EH LL OW]"
SAVE "tpi:say"	Stop speaking
