BASIC Function             STICK
--------------------------------
STICK (part,player)

Read a joystick and return its
state. Player is 1 or 2 for the
left and right joystick ports,
respectively. Part=1 for reading
the stick direction, and part=2
for reading the button state
(1 = pressed, 0 = not pressed).

Direction Values:    5 1 9 
                      \:/  
                     4-0-8 
                      /:\  
Example:             6 2 10
  LET p2s=STICK(1,2)
  LET p2b=STICK(2,2)

This keyword replaced the
vertical bar character that was
in Spectrum BASIC. The character
bit pattern bytes are still in
the ROM.

See also: INKEY$
