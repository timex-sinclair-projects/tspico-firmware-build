TS-Pico Command           PICOPT
--------------------------------
SAVE "tpi:picopt"

Send LPRINT, LLIST and COPY to
the TS-Pico's virtual printer
instead of a real printer. Text
goes to /VLPRINT/PRNnnnn.TXT on
the SD card, COPY pictures to
/VSCREEN/SCRnnnn.BMP.
Printer commands: opprint,
clprint, autolf, noautolf,
autopg, noautopg, prnsz, bmp.

See also: ts2040, lprint, copy
