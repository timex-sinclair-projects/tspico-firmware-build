TS-Pico Command            GREET
--------------------------------
SAVE "tpi:greet"[ text]

The v3 card's greeting: said at
switch-on when the 2068 starts
its own ROM (not after a one-
shot tpi:boot or with a
cartridge in the DOCK).
text: say it now, and from now
  on at switch-on
off:  no greeting (the default)
No text: shows the greeting
Saved in config.ini
(SPEECH_GREETING), which takes a
longer one than BASIC can send.

See also: config
