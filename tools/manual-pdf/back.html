The TS-Pico plugs into the Timex Sinclair 2068 and gives it a micro SD card: LOAD and SAVE in moments instead of minutes, the disk commands CAT, MOVE, ERASE and FORMAT, files and streams you can open from BASIC, a virtual printer, ROM and cartridge slots, and a ZX Spectrum mode.

This manual covers all of it, from fitting the board to every command and report.
